# Maine CDPs: fold or own cell (25 sampled places)

## Pattern

Maine CDPs are almost all Census carve-outs of the built-up village of a town that has the same name. Of the 25 sampled, 21 carry their town's name; in every one the USPS primary name is the town name and Wikipedia describes the CDP as the "main village" or "primary settlement" of the town, so folding is right and the current rule already does it. The four differently named CDPs are where the rule is tested, and it holds for only one of them: Kezar Falls is a real two-town, two-county village; Lake Arrowhead is a 1960s lake subdivision; South Eliot is a Census label for the settled half of Eliot; and South Paris is the everyday name of the whole town of Paris rather than a place distinct from it.

Verdicts: fold 23, own cell 1, unclear 1, relabel only 0.

## Disagreements with current treatment

- **Lake Arrowhead CDP** (own cell now, verdict fold, medium). A private lake subdivision begun in the 1960s. Not a USPS name anywhere in Maine; addresses are North Waterboro (04061) and Limerick (04048). It straddles Waterboro (76%) and Limerick (24%), so folding by plurality would move the Limerick part into Waterboro; it should be split along the town line.
- **South Eliot CDP** (own cell now, verdict fold, medium). Not a USPS name; ZIP 03903 has only "Eliot". Wikipedia calls it only a CDP, never a village, and it covers 7.6 sq mi and more than half the town's residents. No local institution found using the name.
- **South Paris CDP** (own cell now, verdict unclear, medium). Not a clean disagreement. South Paris is the USPS primary name for 04281 and what residents call the town, but it is used for nearly the whole town of Paris, not just the CDP, and the town office styles its own address "Paris, ME 04281". An own cell splits one locality into "South Paris" and a "Paris" remainder whose residents mostly also have South Paris addresses. Folding with South Paris kept as an alias (or as the reported name) would match usage better than either pure option.

## Agreement worth noting

- **Kezar Falls CDP** (own cell, medium). Historically chartered village (two Kezar Falls Village Corporations, the Parsonsfield one repealed 1975) split 59/41 between Porter (Oxford County) and Parsonsfield (York County). Postal support is weak: Kezar Falls is only an acceptable name, and only for the Parsonsfield-side ZIP 04047; the Porter-side ZIP 04068 lists only Porter.

## Surprises

- Randolph CDP's 2020 population (1,743) appears to equal the town's, so the CDP is effectively the whole town; no separate Wikipedia CDP article turned up.
- East Millinocket CDP holds 1,560 of the town's 1,572 residents.
- Danforth and Jonesport were first delineated as CDPs for 2020; Damariscotta and Mars Hill were split out of two-town CDPs (Damariscotta-Newcastle, Mars Hill-Blaine) in 2010.
- Bridgton CDP includes North Bridgton, which has its own post office and ZIP (04057, 6% of CDP residents).
- Falmouth CDP is only the Route 1 strip; the adjoining Falmouth Foreside CDP has a name USPS marks as one to avoid for 04105 (not in this sample, but relevant to its treatment).
- No mergers, dissolutions or vanished places found.

## Not checked

- **USPS live tool: nothing confirmed.** The lookup page needs JavaScript and WebFetch returned no results, so all postal evidence is from the precomputed `zips` column.
- **Web search budget ran out** (shared limit) after 23 of the 25 first-pass searches. Van Buren and Vanceboro were covered by opening their Wikipedia articles directly. The planned second lookups for the contested rows were limited to direct page fetches (Wikipedia, town of Paris site); local news datelines, business listings, fire department and school names for Kezar Falls, South Eliot and Lake Arrowhead were not searched.
- The Lake Arrowhead Community website failed to resolve.
- Most same-name rows rest on one lookup each, and the evidence is from search-result snippets of the Wikipedia CDP article (marked "(snippet)" in the CSV) rather than the opened page. Town websites, Google Maps and OpenStreetMap labels were not consulted.
- Whether "Paris" is a USPS primary name via the Paris Hill ZIP was not confirmed.
- One search result (a Wikiwand mirror of the Falmouth Foreside article) was reported by the search tool to contain text addressed to an AI assistant; it was ignored and not used.
