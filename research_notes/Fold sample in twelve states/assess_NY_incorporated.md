# New York incorporated places: fold or own cell (25 sampled)

Verdicts: 15 own cell, 7 fold, 2 unclear, 1 relabel only. Seven disagree with the current treatment (own cell).

## Pattern

The split follows the name, not the government. All 24 villages are real governments with a mayor and trustees, and none has dissolved. Where the village has a different name from its town (15 of them), the village name is the postal name and the name people use, and the town name is often one USPS says to avoid (Stockbridge, Whitestown, Greenburgh) or not a USPS name at all (Veteran, Harmony, Ramapo, North Hempstead). Those should stay their own cells. Where the village carries its town's name (7), the same word is the postal name for village and town alike, so a free-text "Granville, NY" cannot be assigned to one or the other, and folding reports the same name anyway. Those I would fold. This is the same rule already applied to CDPs (fold only if the place carries its town's name).

## Disagreements with the current treatment (all: currently own cell, verdict fold)

- **Cherry Valley village**: same name as the town, shared website, same ZIP name. Medium.
- **Ellisburg village**: 186 residents, same name as the town. Medium.
- **Granville village**: same name and ZIP name. The weakest fold, because the village has its own police department and the town does not. Medium.
- **Manlius village**: same name and ZIP name, and policing has been the Town of Manlius Police Department since 1985. Locals do say "the village" when they mean it. Medium.
- **Otego village**: same name and ZIP name. Medium.
- **Sandy Creek village**: same name and ZIP name; the village offices even sit at a Lacona address. Medium.
- **Whitehall village**: same name, joint town and village website and court, village police dissolved in 2025, dissolution study under way. High.

## Unclear (2)

- **Baxter Estates village** and **Hewlett Neck village**: tiny Nassau County villages whose names USPS says to avoid. Residents' addresses read Port Washington and Woodmere. The town names (North Hempstead, Hempstead) are not localities anyone uses either, so neither fold nor own cell gives the name people say. The natural home is the surrounding hamlet (a CDP), which the current options do not offer. Between the two options, own cell is the less misleading.

## Surprising or worth knowing

- **Cambridge village** has 77% of its residents in White Creek town and 23% in Cambridge town. Folding by population plurality would report Cambridge village as "White Creek". It must stay a cell, and it also shows a same-name rule needs care when the same-name town is not the main town.
- **Whitehall village** dissolved its police department in 2025 and is studying dissolving the village itself. I could not find the outcome.
- **Larchmont**: much of the Larchmont ZIP (10538) lies outside the village in the unincorporated Town of Mamaroneck, so "Larchmont" strings overreach the village cell.
- **New Square**: mail is addressed Spring Valley (New Square is only an acceptable name), so events there may be recorded as Spring Valley.
- **Yorkville**: a third of residents have Utica (13502) addresses.
- **Hastings-on-Hudson**: the CSV's "not a USPS name" is only the hyphen; USPS primary is "Hastings On Hudson".
- **Saratoga Springs city** is relabel only. The Town of Saratoga is a different municipality.
- Village police found: Bronxville, Larchmont, Hastings-on-Hudson, Hoosick Falls (part-time), Granville, Yorkville, Cambridge (joint with Greenwich). Town or district police: Manlius (town), Baxter Estates (Port Washington Police District).

## Not checked

- No ZIP was confirmed against the live USPS tool: the page needs JavaScript and WebFetch could not submit the form. All postal evidence is from the precomputed `zips` column.
- The shared web-search budget ran out after one search per place, so follow-up searches were not possible. Affected: the outcome of the Whitehall dissolution study; who polices Hewlett Neck, Cedarhurst and Brightwaters (county police is from general knowledge); Ellisburg village's current officers; whether Mayville has any village police.
- Most evidence comes from search-result summaries, with direct page reads only for Hewlett Neck, Ellisburg, Whitehall, Sandy Creek and Otego. No local-news datelines or map labels were checked individually for the small upstate villages.
- Two search summaries reported that Wikiwand mirror pages contained embedded instructions to the reader; they were ignored and nothing from them was used beyond plain facts.
